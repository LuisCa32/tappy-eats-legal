How do I unlock all the restaurants?

Burger Barn and Pizza Planet are free. Taco Town and Noodle Moon unlock together with a single one-time purchase. Tap a locked restaurant, then complete the grown-ups-only gate to reach the purchase screen.

I bought the unlock — how do I restore it on another device?

Open the unlock screen (tap a locked restaurant and pass the parental gate) and tap Restore purchase. As long as you’re signed in with the same Apple Account, your unlock will come right back.

Does Tappy Eats need the internet or collect any data?

No. The game plays fully offline and collects no personal information — no accounts, no ads, no tracking. The only data involved is the record of your optional purchase, handled by Apple. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does Tappy Eats make sounds?

No — the game is silent, so it’s fine to play anywhere. Taps give a gentle vibration instead, which follows your iPhone’s own haptics setting in Settings → Sounds & Haptics.

Is it safe for my child to play alone?

Yes — that’s the whole idea. Play is tap-only with no timers, there are no ads or outside links, and any purchase sits behind a grown-ups-only math question — and the App Store still asks you to confirm before anything is charged.
