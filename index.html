Privacy Policy for Tappy Eats

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Tappy Eats (“the App”) is a pretend food-delivery game for young children, developed by Luis Cabrera (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the App does and does not handle. It is written to be honest and complete — the App is built to be offline and privacy-first.

The short version Tappy Eats works fully offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect any personal information about you or your child. The only data involved is the record of an optional one-time in-app purchase, which is processed by Apple and used only to deliver that purchase.

Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. Specifically:

  • No account, sign-up, name, email address, or phone number is required or requested.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking software of any kind.
  • No behavioral profiling and no data used to track you across apps or websites.
  • All gameplay runs on the device and works without an internet connection.

In-app purchase

The App offers a single optional one-time purchase that unlocks additional restaurants. This purchase is initiated only after an adult passes a press-and-hold parental gate.

The payment itself is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment card, bank details, or billing information.

To deliver, validate, and restore the purchase, we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous identifier and details about the transaction (such as the product purchased and the purchase date). This Purchase History is used only to provide the app’s functionality — unlocking and restoring your purchase. It is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you.

Children’s privacy

Tappy Eats is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. There are no chat features, no social features, no ads, and no links out of the game during play. Any purchase is placed behind a parental gate so it cannot be started by a child alone. If you believe we have somehow received a child’s personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Third-party services

The App relies on these services only for the optional purchase:

  • Apple App Store — processes the payment.
  • RevenueCat — delivers and restores the purchase.

These providers handle the purchase data they receive under their own privacy policies, linked above. We do not use any other third-party services.

Data retention

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Records of your purchase are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their respective policies so that your purchase can be restored on your devices.

Your choices

Since the App collects no personal data, there is no personal profile to access, correct, or delete. You can manage or request a refund for your purchase through your Apple ID / App Store account settings. If you have any questions, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and post the revised policy at this URL. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
